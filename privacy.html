Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 31, 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how [[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]] ("HydroPass", "we"), [[POSTAL ADDRESS]], handles personal information on the HydroPass website and apps. Questions: [[PRIVACY EMAIL]].

What we collect

  • •
    Account: your name, email address and, if you add one, a profile photo. Your password is held by our sign-in provider (Amazon Cognito); we never see it.
  • •
    Hosting: your listings, including the property's exact address and location, photos, rules, prices, availability and access instructions such as gate codes, and the details Stripe needs to pay you (held by Stripe).
  • •
    Bookings and payments: dates, group details, amounts, refunds and payment status. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never receive the card number.
  • •
    What you write: messages and their attachments, reviews, and saved searches and favorites.
  • •
    Searches: the places you type into a location box, and the map area you look at.
  • •
    Near me: if you use it, your browser asks your permission, then gives us your location rounded to about 1 km. We use it only for that search; it isn't saved to your account, but like any search it appears in our server logs for a short time.
  • •
    Technical: your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages and API calls you request, in server logs and for rate limiting and security.
We don't ask for your birth date, and we never collect your precise location.

How we use it

  • •
    To run the service: accounts, listings, search, bookings, payments, refunds, messages and reviews.
  • •
    To send the emails the service needs (booking, payment and trip emails) and, where you've asked for them, alerts such as saved-search updates.
  • •
    To keep HydroPass safe: preventing fraud and abuse, enforcing our terms, and securing accounts. When someone reports a listing, a review or a message, a person on our team reads it, including a reported message and its attachment's name, even though they aren't part of that conversation.
  • •
    To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to run HydroPass:
  • •
    Other users: your name, profile photo and the reviews you write and receive are visible to others. A host sees a renter's booking details and messages; a renter whose booking is paid sees the property's exact location and access details.
  • •
    Stripe: payments, refunds and host payouts, including identity checks Stripe runs on hosts.
  • •
    Amazon Web Services: sign-in (Cognito), email delivery (SES), photo and file storage (S3) and the servers that run HydroPass (Lightsail).
  • •
    Cloudflare: hosts the website and carries every request to our servers, so it processes IP addresses and request data.
  • •
    Geoapify: the text you type in a location search, sent from our servers (not from your browser) to find matching places.
  • •
    Map tiles: when you view a map, your browser loads map images from OpenFreeMap and terrain data from AWS open data, which see your IP address and the area shown.
  • •
    Authorities or others when the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

No selling, no trackers

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We load no advertising, analytics or session-recording tools, and no third party tracks you across sites through HydroPass. Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal or Global Privacy Control: since we don't track you, there is nothing for them to switch off, and if we ever add such a tool, it won't load without your consent and will respect Global Privacy Control.
We use your browser's storage only to keep you signed in and remember your settings, not for advertising.

Your choices and rights

  • •
    See and change your profile at any time on your Profile page.
  • •
    Turn off saved-search alerts and review requests from the Unsubscribe link in those emails, or on your Profile page. Booking, payment and trip emails are always sent.
  • •
    Ask for a copy of your information or deletion of your account from the Privacy section of your Profile page while signed in, or ask for those or a correction by writing to [[PRIVACY EMAIL]] from the email on your account. We answer within 45 days. Some records (bookings, payments, tax records) we must keep for a time even after an account is closed.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and as long as needed to provide the service. After that, we keep booking, payment and tax records for as long as the law and our accounting require, and messages and reviews tied to a booking while they matter to it. Server logs are kept for a short time for security.

Security

Connections are encrypted, gate codes are stored encrypted, and photos and attachments are served through private links where they aren't meant to be public. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

HydroPass is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, write to [[PRIVACY EMAIL]] and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change is material, we will tell you by email or on the site before it takes effect. The Terms of Service say how changes to the terms work.
